# Atrai Animal Civic Primitive Grammar - 2026-05-01

## Status

Draft overlay.

Purpose: make Atrai primitive puzzle pieces read as civic organisms instead of loose robotics parts.

This does not replace the mechanical primitive registry. It is a depiction and teaching layer over the existing registry.

## Contract

The animal form is the experience grammar.

The primitive id remains the truth grammar.

An animal-looking part may help a human understand posture, role, risk, and relationship faster, but it must not upgrade an unproven mechanical fit into a proven one.

## Why This Exists

The current primitive stack is mechanically honest, but it can read as separated robotics hardware:

- coil
- cable
- panel
- cage
- sphere
- bus
- gate

That is useful for engineering inspection, but weak for civic-center experience. The civic center needs recognizable living roles: carrier, shell, sensor, nest, circulatory path, guardian, swarm, and refuge.

## Depiction Rules

1. Animal bodies are allowed only as role-bearing overlays.
2. The card must still show the primitive id and source chain.
3. The fit check remains mechanical: ports, units, anchors, catch path, source, sink, and HOLD.
4. Organic style must not hide risk labels.
5. No animal depiction may imply autonomy over humans.
6. Civic-center scenes should prefer creature clusters over isolated hardware parts.

## Civic Center Puzzle Pieces

The first civic-center puzzle set should read like a small living ecology:

- `phase_spine` becomes the route creature moving through the center.
- `tensile_lines` become sensing filaments that show tension and consent boundaries.
- `cubic_panel_chain` becomes a shell creature that unfolds into shelter, floor, or wall.
- `charge_packet` becomes a tracked rolling life packet, never shown without a catch path.
- `catch_cage` becomes a nest that receives, pauses, and makes the moving packet safe.
- `utility_bus` becomes the circulatory network joining civic service organs.
- `acoustic_no_entry_zone` becomes a listening membrane around spaces, routes, and service organs.
- `source_hold_gate` becomes the visible human-held boundary around the whole scene.

## Acoustic Boundary Primitive

Acoustic engineering is a first-class civic primitive, not an after-effect.

The no-entry zone is the boundary where sound, attention, proximity, and ingress are shaped before a person, organism, or machine crosses into a sensitive area.

Valid uses:

- warning before unsafe entry
- privacy around care, governance, sleep, archive, and recovery spaces
- sensory-soft routing for children, elders, injured people, and overloaded operators
- public ceremony and attention shaping
- public humor pulses for tone, consent, and friction sensing
- diagnostic pulse for route state, door state, or service state
- nonverbal "do not cross yet" signaling

Not valid:

- coercive sonic force
- hidden persuasion
- pain or disorientation as a control method
- pretending an audio cue is consent or authority

Required fit checks:

- source speaker or acoustic surface
- listener/receiver class
- safe intensity band
- duration and duty cycle
- ingress rule
- fallback visual/tactile cue
- Source HOLD when tied to real people, public instructions, or access control

## Brotology Public Humor Surface

The grassroots brotology front humorists are a public-facing operational surface.

Their job is not command. Their job is social acoustic sensing: use jokes, music, chant, timing, and shared laughter to test whether a public moment is open, tense, overloaded, confused, too solemn, or ready to move.

They work with surfology/acoustic experts to read depth:

- where laughter opens the room
- where the joke lands too hard
- where silence means overload
- where seriousness is protective
- where seriousness is performative friction
- where the acoustic membrane needs a softer cue

Allowed outputs:

- aggregate mood notes
- friction watch
- timing notes
- public-surface readiness
- banger/laughter prompt candidates
- acoustic no-entry or slow-entry recommendations

Not allowed:

- personal blacklists
- harassment routing
- humiliating private people
- treating "too serious" as guilt
- converting laughter pressure into authority

The primitive name for this lane is:

`public_laughter_sonar`

It belongs under `acoustic_no_entry_zone`, `attention_beacon`, and `community_consent`, not under command.

## UI Consequence

The organism-mainframe should stop presenting the primitive puzzle as raw robotics parts by default.

Default depiction should be:

`civic animal ecology`

Inspection mode may still expose:

`mechanical primitive registry`

The operator should be able to switch between:

- `animal`: civic organism depiction
- `mechanical`: ports, units, fit checks
- `source`: docs, receipts, HOLD

## Failure Labels

Use existing failure labels, but phrase them on the animal surface without hiding the engineering problem:

- `unanchored`: loose limb / no anchor
- `unsourced`: unknown creature / no source
- `unbounded`: overgrown / no limit
- `unreceipted`: no trail / no provenance
- `uncaught`: runaway packet / no nest
- `unsunk`: flowing nowhere / no sink
- `ungated`: no guardian / needs Source HOLD
- `cosmetic`: decorative only / no primitive package

## Acceptance Test

The depiction passes when a non-specialist can answer:

1. What kind of civic animal am I looking at?
2. What primitive id does it map to?
3. What does it carry, sense, protect, route, or authorize?
4. What must be true before it is allowed to connect?
5. Where is the Source HOLD boundary?

If the answer is only "robot part," the depiction failed.

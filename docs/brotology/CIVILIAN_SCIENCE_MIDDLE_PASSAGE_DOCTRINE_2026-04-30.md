Date: 2026-04-30
Status: active Brotology doctrine

## Purpose

Name the Middle Passage package as a civilian-science surface inside
Brotology: a public method for imagining, bounding, testing, and revising
historically plausible oceanographic hypotheses without making discovery claims.

This document exists so the meme energy, package energy, and memorial energy do
not drift into overclaiming.

## Core Law

```text
Dream loud.
Claim quiet.
Receipts decide.
Do not publish the treasure map.
Publish the compass, the ethics, and the audit trail.
```

## Civilian Science

Civilian science is not fake science and not institutional cosplay.

It is the discipline of a public operator who:

- asks a real question
- builds a reproducible tool
- states assumptions plainly
- shows uncertainty
- invites audit
- refuses to turn simulation into proof

The operator may dream the path first. The package must make that dream
inspectable, bounded, and correctable.

## Middle Passage Package Role

The package is allowed to model:

- voyage-record hypotheses
- wind/current assumptions
- drift and sinking-delay ranges
- bathymetry/depth context
- scatter and uncertainty radius
- generalized survey-priority zones
- memorial-protection GIS outputs

The package is not allowed to claim:

- remains were found
- exact burial locations are known
- a public map authorizes recovery, salvage, or disturbance
- a model output outranks descendant/community governance or expert review

## Layering

Public layer:

- story
- ethics
- generalized zones
- open methods
- reproducible code
- source and assumption receipts

Restricted layer:

- exact coordinates
- source uncertainty details
- live hypotheses
- sensitive voyage-event joins
- candidate site precision

Governance layer:

- descendant/community review
- historical source review
- maritime archaeology review
- oceanography review
- legal/protection review

## Claim Protocol

Allowed public wording:

- `theoretical memorial modeling`
- `survey-priority zone`
- `generalized protection zone`
- `historically plausible hypothesis`
- `reproducible assumption trail`
- `not proof of remains`

Avoid public wording:

- `we found them`
- `grave location`
- `treasure map`
- `salvage target`
- `confirmed remains`
- `exact resting place`
